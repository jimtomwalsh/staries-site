Write down your work wins while they’re fresh. Staries shapes each one the way interviewers expect, then helps you practise it out loud.

Built around STAR, the shape interviewers listen for.

STAR is how interviewers expect these answers to run. Staries asks you for each part in turn.

S
Situation: where you were and what was going on
T
Task: what you had to do
A
Action: what you did, step by step
R
Result: what changed. A number helps.
